### Bounded prefix realizers = positive-integer realizability

sup_N r_N < infinity  <=>  the infinite valuation word has a positive-integer realizer  <=>  r_N is eventually constant. Unconditional, and independent of the Curry sector. Consequence: qualitative unbounded-realizer growth is NOT an easier target than positive-integer realizability, and must not be pursued as if it were.

### Least prefix realizer stabilizes

Bounded least-prefix-realizers are eventually constant. For an actual positive seed m_0 this means r_N = m_0 once 2^{S_N+1} > m_0 -- an extremely rigid signature, but not a mechanism for forbidding stabilization.

- **source:** EOC/ZCRERealizerGrowth.lean · `EOC.ZCRERealizerGrowth.leastRealizer_eventually_constant_of_bounded`
