*** TASK 2 MAIN FINDING (COMPUTATIONAL, verified for all 16 betas x 2 J, T2_THETA.txt) ***
The convergence Theta_beta -> Theta_alpha is NOT asymptotic, it is a DICHOTOMY:

    Theta_beta == Theta_alpha bit-for-bit   <=>   floor(j*beta) == floor(j*alpha) for all j <= J.

Verified as an iff on every (beta, J) pair.  This is obvious once stated -- the model only
ever reads top[0..J] -- but it means the "rate relative to |beta-alpha|" question has no
continuous answer.  When agreement fails, the observed ratio |dTheta|/|b-a| is
0.05-0.12 at J=400 and 0.008-0.034 at J=800, i.e. the perturbation per barrier index is
bounded and DECREASES with J: the barrier perturbation is a boundary/transient effect,
not a bulk one.  Prefix length m is the wrong variable; the right one is
first_diff(beta) vs J, and first_diff grows like the partial-quotient denominators, not
like exp(m).

*** TASK 6 CONCLUSION (COMPUTATIONAL) ***
The substitution exists and is clean, but the superblocks it produces (5-6 blocks) are
roughly 4x TOO SHORT to carry a pressure certificate: a uniform blockwise certificate

superblock types cannot work; the state would have to encode ~24 blocks of black pattern.
