8d. LOWER BOUND ON |lambda| FOR A PHASE IN THE EXCEPTIONAL SET
==============================================================================
  The window fixes m = sg + t + 1; the environment is xi = lambda (cw2env parametrisation),
  and moving to a window whose geometry dictates m + delta is xi -> lambda * 2^{-delta}.
  Realizing the 64-level pattern therefore requires
       lambda ≡ e * 2^{delta}  (mod 3^138)   for one of the 16 e in E.

  min over E of |lambda| at delta = 0 : 8628357598004709039287903785445954915294778314402654274318196576  (64 digits)
  min over 0 <= delta <= 2000        : 34552445086067519557312068563506237913904168472350853305790373  (62 digits) at delta = 676
  DIGIT FLOOR over that delta range  : 62 decimal digits

  RIGOROUS (exact-arithmetic) statement, no genericity assumed:
    for every delta in [0,2000] and every e in E, min(|e*2^delta|, 3^138-|.|) >= 10^61.
    Hence no |lambda| <= 10^61 realizes the full pattern at any of those 2001 window
    geometries.  (This is a finite exact check, not a density argument.)

  COUNTING BOUND (HEURISTIC, stated as such): over delta in [0,D] the set of admissible

  L = 10^3 one needs D >= 3^138/(32*10^3) - 1 ~ 1.4e+61.
